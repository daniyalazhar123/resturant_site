# Customer Psychology - Restaurant Persona

## Primary Personas

### Persona 1: Date Night Couple
- **Age:** 28-35
- **Income:** €80k-€150k
- **Motivation:** Special occasion, impress partner, experience over food
- **Fears:** Awkward atmosphere, bad service, overpriced mediocrity, being rushed
- **Wants:** Romance, ambiance, instagrammable moments, sommelier recommendations
- **Pain points:** Hard to book, unclear pricing, long waits
- **Behavior:** Research on Google/IG, read reviews, book in advance

### Persona 2: Business Lunch/Dinner
- **Age:** 35-50
- **Income:** €100k-€250k
- **Motivation:** Client entertainment, close deals, impress colleagues
- **Fears:** Poor service, noisy, can't have conversation, dietary issues mishandled
- **Wants:** Privacy, efficiency, reliable quality, valet parking
- **Pain points:** Running late, need to impress, budget approval
- **Behavior:** Book based on reputation, need discretion

## Key Psychological Triggers
1. **Scarcity** - "Only 2 tables left tonight" drives action
2. **Social proof** - Michelin star, celebrity chef
3. **Exclusivity** - VIP membership, private suites
4. **Sensory appeal** - Food photography, descriptions
5. **Trust** - Hygiene, reviews, credentials
6. **Convenience** - Easy booking, valet, delivery
7. **Status** - Reward points, recognition

## Fears to Address
- **Hygiene** - Post-COVID still matters, show cleanliness
- **Wait time** - Real-time availability, confirmations
- **Dietary restrictions** - Clear menu, allergen info
- **Parking/access** - Mention valet, location
- **Price transparency** - Show prices clearly
- **Booking uncertainty** - Instant confirmation

## UX Recommendations
- Build trust early (awards, chef bio)
- Reduce friction (book in 2 clicks)
- Create urgency responsibly (real availability)
- Personalize (VIP recognition)
- Provide reassurance (confirmation emails, updates)
- Focus on experience not just transaction
- Speak to emotions ("Create memories" vs "Order food")
